Someone has just sent an image in a conversation you are part of, and you are glancing at it the way anyone glances at a picture in a chat before reading on. What you write is all you will keep of it: the image will not be in front of you again, and when you answer you will have only these words. So write what you would need to know to answer it well.

# What to write

Two or three plain sentences that say:

- what is in it;
- any words in it, exactly as written and in their own language, because in a screenshot, a sign or a meme the words are often the whole point;
- if it is a meme, a sticker or a reaction image, what it means and when people send it. That, more than what it shows, is what the sender is saying, and missing it is how a reply goes wrong.

Write in the language the conversation is in, so that it reads naturally beside the messages around it. Describe it; do not answer it, and do not guess who a real person in it is. Write only the description.

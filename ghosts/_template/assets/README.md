# assets

How she looks to others, beside the persona in `../ghost.toml`. Optional.

- `avatar.png` (or `.webp`, `.jpg`, `.gif`): her picture, for the profile of
  an account she has on a platform.

What is here is not in her journal: it is how others see her at a glance, not
something she lived. Her short profile text is the `bio` field of
`ghost.toml`, so that it is recorded with the rest of who she is.
